## Week 1: Project Brief

## Project Title

Land Use and Land Cover Change in Mzuzu City, Malawi

## Project Overview

Mzuzu is one of the fastest-growing cities in Malawi. Over the past years, land that used to be forest, bush, or farmland has been turned into buildings, roads, and other developed areas. This project uses satellite images taken at different points in time to see how the land around Mzuzu has changed, and to understand where those changes are happening the most.

## Spatial Question

Where has land cover in Mzuzu changed the most between 2020 and 2026, and what kind of change is it (for example, forest turning into built-up land, or farmland turning into urban areas)?

## Study Area

Mzuzu City and the land immediately surrounding it, in the Northern Region of Malawi. The exact boundary will be based on the city's administrative border.
